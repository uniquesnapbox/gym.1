Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my membership anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your membership with 30 days notice. There are no long-term contracts or hidden fees.

Is there a joining fee?

Currently, we're waiving the $50 joining fee for all new members. This is a limited-time offer!

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes, you can freeze your membership for up to 3 months per year for medical or personal reasons.

Do you offer student discounts?

Yes! Students with valid ID receive 15% off all membership plans.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and automatic bank transfers for your convenience.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Absolutely! You can change your membership level at any time, effective the following billing cycle.
